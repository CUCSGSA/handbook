# Election Operating Procedure
## Election Committee
- Executive members of CSGSA and CSGPDC form the Election Committee.
- Committee members re-running must excuse themselves from election committee to avoid conflicts of interest.
- If every executive member wishes to re-run, good-faith effort should be made to appoint a neutral third party to administer the election.
## Stages of the Election
### 1. Self-Nomination (2 weeks)
- **Form Duration**: Open for two weeks.
- **Information Collected**:
  - Degree type (e.g., PhD, MS)
  - Reasons for running and areas of interest
- **Committee Confirmation**: The Election Committee may reach out as a good-faith effort to ensure nominees understand the roles and responsibilities.
### 2. Voting (1 week)
- **Form Duration**: Open for one week.
- **Structure**: Each vacant position is a separate question; options list self-nominated candidates.
- **Eligibility Verification**:
  - Voters must affirm they are current CS graduate students and eligible to vote; misrepresentation may be referred to the CU Honor Council.
  - Email addresses collected will be validated by the department or an advisor to confirm status.
## Data Privacy & Integrity
- The CSGSA executive committee may share voter information **only** to ensure vote validity; individual data must not be used or disseminated for other purposes.
## Tie-Breaking Procedures
- In the event of a tie, the CSGSA executive committee will decide a tie-breaker method (e.g., ranked voting, volunteer history, vote of current/future leaders or staff).
- The chosen tie-breaker mechanism should be publicly disclosed when announcing the winner.
## Suggestions for Future Elections
- Limit self-nominations to a maximum of three positions, with ranked preferences.
- Encourage students to nominate for only one position to focus on what matters most to them.
- At the start of the voting form, include a clear affirmation of eligibility and consequences for dishonesty.
- State that nominees or voters who share the form with ineligible participants will face disqualification.
